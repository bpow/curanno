De novo SCN1A variant in an infant with prolonged febrile seizures

Clinical report

The proband, a girl, had her first seizure at 6 months of age during a fever. She went on to have prolonged febrile and afebrile seizures, and developmental delay became apparent in her second year. She had no ataxia and her brain MRI was normal.

A chromosomal microarray performed at 1 year was normal. A gene panel for epilepsy then identified a heterozygous SCN1A variant, NM_001165963.4:c.2836C>T (p.Arg946Cys), which was absent in both parents.

There was no family history of epilepsy; the parents are not consanguineous.
