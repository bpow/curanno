Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy in two siblings with a MYH7 missense variant

Abstract

We describe two siblings with early-onset hypertrophic cardiomyopathy who carry the same heterozygous MYH7 variant.

Case presentation

Patient 1, a 14-year-old boy, presented with exertional dyspnea and syncope during sport. Echocardiography showed asymmetric septal hypertrophy with a septal thickness of 24 mm. He had no arrhythmia on Holter monitoring.

Exome sequencing identified the heterozygous variant NM_000257.4:c.1208G>A (p.Arg403Gln) in MYH7, ClinVar Variation ID 14089.

Patient 2, his 11-year-old sister, was evaluated after her brother's diagnosis. She reported palpitations and had left ventricular hypertrophy on echocardiography. Cascade testing by Sanger sequencing confirmed that she carries the same MYH7 variant.

Their father had died suddenly at 38 years; no DNA was available from him.

Discussion

The variant is absent from gnomAD and has been reported in several families with hypertrophic cardiomyopathy.
