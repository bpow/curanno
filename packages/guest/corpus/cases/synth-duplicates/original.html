Sample intake protocol

Each sample is logged on arrival, labelled, and stored according to its type. Deviations are recorded in the intake log and reviewed weekly.

Section 4.1 Blood

Store at 4 °C within 30 minutes of arrival. Centrifuge within 2 hours. Record the time of centrifugation in the intake log.

Section 4.2 Saliva

Store at room temperature for up to 24 hours. Do not freeze. Record the collection time in the intake log.

Section 4.3 Tissue

Store at −80 °C within 15 minutes of arrival. Record the freezer position in the intake log.

Section 5.2 Shipping

Samples leaving the building travel on dry ice. Record the courier reference in the intake log.

Sign-off

The intake supervisor signs the log at the end of each shift. Unsigned logs are escalated to the laboratory manager the next morning.
